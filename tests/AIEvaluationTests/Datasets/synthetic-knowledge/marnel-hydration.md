# Marnel Hydration Notes

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. It describes invented things and is used only to test retrieval. It is not medical information.

## Daily fluids

Adults in the Marnel notes were advised to drink about two litres of fluids per day. More is needed in hot weather or during exercise. Water, milk, tea and soups all count towards the total, and the notes say that thirst is a useful but late signal.

## Signs of dehydration

Dark yellow urine, thirst, headache and dizziness can be signs of mild dehydration. Pale straw coloured urine usually means that fluid intake is adequate. Severe symptoms such as confusion or fainting need urgent medical help.

## Marnel electrolyte salts

Marnel salts are a fictional product that contains sodium and potassium. The notes say that electrolyte drinks are only needed after long exercise above 90 minutes or after illness with vomiting. For short workouts plain water is enough.
