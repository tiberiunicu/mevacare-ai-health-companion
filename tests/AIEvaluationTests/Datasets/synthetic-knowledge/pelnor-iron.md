# Pelnor Iron Facts

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. It describes invented things and is used only to test retrieval. It is not medical information.

## Why the body needs iron

Iron carries oxygen in red blood cells. Low iron can cause fatigue, pale skin and shortness of breath during light activity. Women who menstruate and people who eat little meat are mentioned as groups that should pay attention to iron.

## Food sources

Red meat, lentils, spinach and fortified cereals contain iron. Vitamin C helps absorb iron from plant foods, so adding citrus fruit or peppers to a lentil meal is helpful.

## Tea and coffee

Tea and coffee with meals can reduce iron absorption. The Pelnor facts suggest drinking them between meals instead.

## Supplements and doctors

See a doctor before taking iron supplements, because too much iron can be harmful. A blood test can show whether iron is actually low.
