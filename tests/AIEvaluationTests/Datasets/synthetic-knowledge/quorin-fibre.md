# Quorin Fibre Handbook

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. It describes invented things and is used only to test retrieval. It is not medical information.

## Daily target

The Quorin handbook recommends 25 grams of fibre per day for women and 38 grams for men. Most people in the handbook's survey ate far less than that. Reaching the target supports regular digestion and a feeling of fullness after meals.

## Rich sources

Oats, lentils, beans, apples with skin and chia seeds are rich in fibre. Whole grain bread contains more fibre than white bread. Vegetables and fruit eaten with their skins add to the total.

## Increasing intake

Increase fibre gradually over two to three weeks and drink more water to reduce bloating. A sudden jump in fibre can cause gas and discomfort. Spreading fibre rich foods across all meals is easier on the gut than eating them all at once.
