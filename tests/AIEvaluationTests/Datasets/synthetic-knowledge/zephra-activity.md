# Zephra Activity Primer

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. It describes invented things and is used only to test retrieval. It is not medical information.

## Weekly activity

Adults in the Zephra primer should aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity per week, or 75 minutes of vigorous activity. Brisk walking, cycling on flat ground and dancing are examples of moderate activity. The minutes can be spread over several days.

## Strength training

Strength training on two days per week should work all major muscle groups: legs, hips, back, chest, shoulders and arms. Body weight exercises such as squats and push ups count. Rest at least one day between hard sessions for the same muscles.

## Daily steps

A daily step goal of 7,000 to 10,000 steps is a practical target in the primer. Short walks after meals are an easy way to add steps. The primer stresses that any increase over the current level is beneficial.

## Warm up

Warm up for five to ten minutes with easy movement before intense exercise, and cool down afterwards with gentle stretching.
